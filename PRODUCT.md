# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Founders and engineering / design / ops leaders at startups and scale-ups who need senior help fast. Their context: they're weighing how to add capacity, comparing an extended team against hiring in-house, going to an agency, using freelancers, or offshoring. They're skeptical (they've been burned by unreliable talent) and time-pressed. The job to be done: get a trustworthy, vetted, timezone-aligned team working on real product in days, without a months-long hiring cycle.

## Product Purpose
Valgo Connect pairs companies with senior, pre-vetted engineers, designers, and operators (Macedonia-based, timezone-aligned, contract-ready in days). The marketing site exists to build trust quickly and convert the visitor into a "Find your team" inquiry. Success = the visitor believes the talent is genuinely senior and vetted, understands how it beats the alternatives, and starts a request.

## Brand Personality
Energetic and human. Warm, fast-moving, people-first, confident without hype. Voice is specific and plainspoken, never buzzword-y. Lime is punctuation (energy, highlights), teal is the trustworthy ground. Motion signals momentum, not decoration.

## Anti-references
- Generic agency template: stocky, soulless, identical-card grids.
- Crypto / AI hype: glows, glassmorphism, gradient text, neon.
- Cheap freelancer marketplace: cluttered, busy, low-trust (Upwork/Fiverr energy).
- Corporate / sterile: cold blue corporate stock, lifeless enterprise look.

## Design Principles
- **People over product shots.** Show real humans doing real work; the talent is the product.
- **Trust through specificity.** Concrete roles, companies, and outcomes beat adjectives.
- **Momentum, with restraint.** Motion conveys speed and energy, but never tips into busy or chaotic.
- **Lime as punctuation.** Accent for highlights and calls to action, not as a wash.
- **Earn the click.** Every section moves a skeptical buyer one step toward "Find your team".

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (entrance reveals and hover effects degrade to instant/crossfade). Visible keyboard focus on every interactive element. Touch targets ≥44px.
